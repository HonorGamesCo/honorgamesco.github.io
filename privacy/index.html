Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Honor Games ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes mobile games, including Vira-Letra and other titles referred to collectively as "our games". This Privacy Policy explains what information our games collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By downloading or using any of our games, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our games.

Information We Collect

For all games

Game Progress & Settings

Saved locally on your device by default (for example: current puzzle state, sound preferences, and statistics). If a game offers an optional account (see below), some of this progress may also be stored on our servers so it can sync across your devices.

Device Information

Anonymous device and usage data collected for analytics and crash reporting purposes.

For games with an account (sign-in)

Account Information

Some of our games let you optionally create an account to save your progress in the cloud and appear on leaderboards. If you choose to sign in, we (through Google Firebase Authentication) collect:

  • Your e-mail address, if you sign in with e-mail and password
  • Your name and e-mail address, if you sign in with Google
  • A unique account identifier

If you don't create an account, you can still play — your progress simply stays on your device and won't sync or appear on any leaderboard.

For games with leaderboards / cloud results

Gameplay Results

When you complete a challenge in a game that has an online leaderboard, we store your result (for example: moves, time, and completion date), your chosen display name, and your account identifier using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so we can show rankings and keep your history in sync.

For games with ads (Google AdMob)

Advertising Data

Google AdMob may collect, on our behalf, in order to show ads:

  • Advertising identifier
  • General location information
  • IP address
  • Interaction with ads
For games with in-app purchases / subscriptions

Purchase Information

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive purchase/subscription status to unlock premium features, but we never see or store your payment card details.

Third-Party Services

Our games may use the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — Google Ads Privacy & Terms
  • Google Play Services (Sign-In & In-App Purchases) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Social Sharing — when you use a share feature, you're using your device's native sharing tools. We don't access or store the content you share.

Data Storage & Security

Local storage: game progress, settings, and preferences that aren't tied to an account are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game removes this data.

Cloud storage: if a game offers an account, sign-in and gameplay results described above are stored on Google Firebase's servers, used exclusively to provide account sync, leaderboards, and subscription status.

Security: we use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Your Choices & Controls

  • Delete your account: contact us at the e-mail below to request deletion of your account and associated cloud data.
  • Sign out / play without an account: your progress stays local-only on your device.
  • Reset advertising ID: available in your device's settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: available in your device's settings under "Ads".
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions: through your Google Play account settings.
  • Remove local data: uninstall the game.

Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it as soon as possible. Parents or guardians who believe their child may have provided us with personal information can contact us using the details below.

Offline Functionality

Many of our games can be played without an internet connection. An internet connection is only required for features such as: signing in and syncing progress, viewing or submitting leaderboard results, displaying ads, and processing in-app purchases.

Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
