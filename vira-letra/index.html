How it works

Vira-Letra takes the classic sliding tile puzzle and gives it a twist: instead of rebuilding a picture, you rebuild words. Every row of the board holds a scrambled word — slide the tiles into the empty space to put every letter back where it belongs. Solve every row and the puzzle is complete.

A brand-new puzzle drops every day, each with its own theme, so there's always something new to untangle. Fewer moves and less time earn you a better score — perfect for a quick daily brain workout.
