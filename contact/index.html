Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug in Vira-Letra?＋

Email us at honorgames.co@gmail.com with your device model and a description of what happened. Screenshots or a short screen recording help us fix it faster.

Do you accept theme or puzzle suggestions?＋

Yes! We love hearing ideas for new daily puzzle themes. Send them to our email and we may feature your idea in a future puzzle.

How can I remove ads?＋

Vira-Letra Premium removes ads through a one-time purchase available inside the game, processed securely by Google Play Billing.

Do I need an account to play?＋

No — you can play fully offline with progress saved on your device. Creating a free account is optional and lets you sync progress and appear on the leaderboard.

Are your games safe for children?＋

Yes. Our games are designed for a general audience and we follow strict content guidelines to keep the experience family-friendly.
